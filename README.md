# Seagrass Lab

Two browser-based teaching tools for the Project FISHES "Saving a Lagoon" challenge, built for students doing seagrass research.

- **Saving a Lagoon** (`index.html`): teams spend a yearly restoration budget on nutrient reduction, seagrass planting, shellfish and algal bloom control, then watch ten years of nitrogen, water clarity, seagrass, shellfish, fish and the local economy respond. Includes stakeholder scorecards, weather replicates, adjustable model parameters and CSV export.
- **Seagrass Tank Lab** (`tank-lab.html`): a greenhouse mesocosm with the lab's 240, 120 and 80 gallon tanks. Students dose hydrogen peroxide and nutrients, track nitrogen, phosphorus, salinity, dissolved oxygen and magnesium, deal with evaporation, wall algae, transplant die-back and equipment failures, and test what their design can and cannot show.

Both are single self-contained HTML files. The tank lab loads three.js r128 from cdnjs for its 3D view; everything else works without it.
